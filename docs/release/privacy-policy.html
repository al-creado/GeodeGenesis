Ads and consent

The game uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions we ask for your consent with Google's consent form before requesting ads, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy options when it is shown. You can also turn off ads in Settings. On Android you can reset or opt out of personalized advertising in your device's Google settings. See how Google uses data from ads.

Cloud save

When cloud save is available, your progress is stored under a random anonymous ID. Because there is no account, uninstalling the game can remove your access to that cloud copy.

How long we keep data

Cloud save data is kept until you ask us to delete it or the game stops operating. Data held by Google for ads and analytics follows Google's own retention policies.

Your choices and rights

You can clear the game's data on your device at any time (Settings > Reset progress, or uninstall the game). To ask for your cloud save to be deleted, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] and include the anonymous ID if you can; we cannot identify a save without it. Depending on where you live you may also have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to certain processing; contact us to exercise them.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will help remove any cloud data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
