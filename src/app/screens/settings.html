Pity rules

  • An Emerald or better is guaranteed on crack number {{ cfg.pity.emeraldOrBetter }} if you haven't gotten one.
  • A Ruby or better is guaranteed on crack number {{ cfg.pity.rubyOrBetter }} if you haven't gotten one.
  • Each counter resets when that rarity or better drops. A Ruby or Diamond resets both.
  • If both guarantees are due on the same crack, you get the Ruby or better.
